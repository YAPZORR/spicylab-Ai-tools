Spicylab API, version 3

Sign in and sign up screens

Eight screens. Each desktop screen (1920 × 1080) sits beside its mobile version (390 wide). The form stays on the left. The right side is now an image banner: one featured image in a glowing frame, a tilted wall of images behind it, and the API details and steps from the homepage on top. On mobile the same images sit in a short banner under the logo.

The banners use 13 AI images. Each one is stored once, as a class from .im1 to .im13 at the end of the styles, so replacing one image there changes it everywhere it appears. Every image place is portrait 3:4: the featured image shows at 400 × 533 and the wall tiles at 228 × 304. On the sign in screens the featured image is blurred behind a lock; it shows clearly on sign up and once the account is created.
