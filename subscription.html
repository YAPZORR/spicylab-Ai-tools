Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your subscription

What are credits? ⌃
Credits are used to generate AI content. Each task uses credits based on its complexity, and subscription credits refresh every billing cycle according to your plan.
Can I cancel anytime? ⌃
Yes. You can cancel from your account settings. Your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period and you will not be charged again.
Do credits roll over? ⌃
Unused subscription credits roll over up to the plan limit. Additional one-time purchased credits never expire.
What payment methods do you accept? ⌃
We accept major credit and debit cards, including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. Payments are processed securely.
What happens to my credits if I upgrade my plan? ⌃
Remaining credits from the current cycle carry over, and your new plan credits are added on top.
