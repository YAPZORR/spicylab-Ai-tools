Buying a plan while signed outNEW

A visitor who isn’t signed in clicks a plan’s button on Plans & Pricing. They create an account or sign in with that plan kept, pay, and land in the Console with the plan active. Pro is used as the example plan.

1Plans & PricingA signed-out visitor clicks Choose Pro.Existing page
2Create account or sign inPro stays selected. New visitors create an account, returning ones switch to Sign in.See both screens below 3CheckoutThey pay for Pro. The plan can still be changed here.See screen 4SubscribedPlan is active. The next step is creating an API key.See screen
5ConsoleDashboard with the plan active and keys unlocked.Existing page

Already signed in with no plan: choosing a plan on Plans & Pricing, or anywhere in the Console, skips step 2 and opens Checkout.

Signs in and already has a plan: skips Checkout and opens Billing to switch plans, so nobody pays twice.
