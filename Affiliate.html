Questions

When does someone count as my referral?

When they create a new SpicyLab account within 24 hours of clicking your link. If they click several affiliate links, the last valid one before they register gets the referral. Once set, it can't change.

When can I withdraw my commission?

Each commission becomes available 30 calendar days after payment, as long as the order isn't refunded, charged back, or under review. You can request a withdrawal once your available balance reaches $10.00. Pending commissions don't count toward it.

How long do payouts take, and who pays the fees?

Payouts are generally completed within 15 business days after a complete and valid request is received. Transaction fees are deducted from your withdrawal, and you'll see the estimated fee and net payout before you confirm.

What happens if a purchase is refunded?

A full refund cancels that commission. A partial refund recalculates it based on what the customer still paid. If the commission was already paid out, the difference is deducted from your future balance.

Why is a commission marked Held?

Our team is reviewing the related payment. A held commission can't be withdrawn until the review is finished, after which it's either released or cancelled.
