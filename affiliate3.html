Common questions

Can any SpicyLab user get a referral link?

No. Links are only for approved creator and media partners with accepted terms. You don’t need to buy anything to apply.

How does attribution work?

When someone clicks your link, we record it on our servers. If they create a new account within 90 days, they’re attributed to you. If they clicked several partners’ links, the last valid click before sign-up counts. Once they register, it can’t change.

When do I get paid?

Monthly. Qualifying commissions from the previous month are processed within the first seven days of the following month. If you’re under the minimum payout, your balance carries over and never expires.

More

Which payout currencies do you support?

USDC and USDT. You choose the currency and a supported network, then add a matching wallet address.

What if my partnership is suspended?

Your links stop tracking, you can’t create new ones and no further commissions are settled. Your history, the reason and the date stay visible in your dashboard, and you can still update your payout details.

Are trial credits the same as commission?

No. Trial credits let you test SpicyLab. They can’t be withdrawn and don’t earn commission, and we track them separately from your cash earnings.

Qualifying purchases

First subscription purchases, subscription renewals and coin packs, for as long as your partnership is active. There’s no 12-month limit.

Message packs, trial credits and your own purchases don’t qualify.

Attribution

A new account created within 90 days of a click on your link is attributed to you. If several partner links were clicked, the last valid click before sign-up counts. Once the account is registered, attribution can’t be overwritten.

Existing accounts, self-referrals and fraudulent sign-ups or traffic never qualify. Attribution is recorded and checked on our servers.

Settlement

Monthly. Qualifying commissions from the previous month are processed within the first seven days of the following month.

Each month we generate a statement, review and approve it, then send payment. A statement is marked Paid only after the transfer is confirmed.

Minimum payout

$9.99, the current price of SpicyLab’s lowest-priced package. It can change, and your dashboard always shows the current amount.

If a month’s balance is under the minimum, it carries over to the next month and never expires.

Refunds and chargebacks

Each commission stays pending for a 7-day refund review period after payment.

A refund or chargeback before payout reverses the commission. After payout, the amount is recovered from your future earnings.
