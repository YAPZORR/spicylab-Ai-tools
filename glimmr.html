Terms of service

Last updated 20 October 2026

Content rules

Use Glimmr only for content you have the right to make. Don’t create or upload sexual content, anything involving minors in a harmful way, or anything that harasses, deceives or impersonates someone.

Real people and consent

Upload a photo of a real person only if they’ve agreed to it being animated or changed, and to how you’ll use the result. Being photographed isn’t the same as agreeing to be animated.

Credits

  • Credits have no cash value and can’t be transferred.
  • If a generation fails on our side or is stopped by our safety check, its credits come back automatically.
  • Without a plan, unused credits pause after 30 days of no activity. Buying any plan or pack makes them active again.

How long results are kept

Results are kept based on the plan you have now, counted from when each one finished:

No plan2 days
Basic30 days
Pro90 days
VIP120 days

When a plan ends, everything in your library moves to the 2-day window. Uploaded photos are deleted about 2 hours after upload — kept 7 days if a generation failed, and 30 days for ECO videos.
