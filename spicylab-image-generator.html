Prompt tips

What moves the result the most, in order.

  • Lead with the subject. Models weigh the first words most, so name who or what is in the frame before the setting.
  • Name a lens or a medium. “35mm film, shallow depth of field” changes the look. “Masterpiece, 8K” doesn’t.
  • Quote the words you want printed. Use Qwen Image for lettering and keep each line to a few words.
  • Draft cheap, finish sharp. Find the framing on Z-Image Turbo, then run the keeper on Spicy Verse 3.0.

Mistakes that waste a run

Each of these costs a full image to find out.

  • A vague subject. “A person in a city” gets you a stock photo. Say who, what they wear and where the light comes from.
  • An open head count. Ask for “two friends” without giving each one an action and you may get three, or one merged face.
  • Picking the ratio last. Cropping a 16:9 frame into a 9:16 story cuts what you composed. Set the ratio first.
  • Paying for 4K drafts. Check framing and faces at 1K, then rerun the one you like at a bigger size.

Questions

Before you run your first image

How much does an image cost?

It depends on the model and the size. Prices start at $0.006 an image, and the Generate button shows the exact total for your settings before you run. It comes out of your balance; there’s no subscription and no credits to convert.

Which model makes the most realistic photos?

Spicy Verse 3.0 and Seedream 5.0 Pro hold up best on portraits and fashion with long, detailed prompts. Run the same prompt on both; your prompt stays when you switch, and the price updates.

Can it put readable text in the picture?

Yes. Pick Qwen Image 3.0, put the exact words in quotes and keep each line short. Check the spelling in the result before you export.

Who can see my images?

Only you. An image is shown to other people only if you publish it as a community template.

Can I turn an image into a video?

Press Make video on any result. Your image becomes the first frame, and you describe what should move. Video is priced per second, shown on the button.

What am I not allowed to make?

Anything involving minors or anyone who could pass for one, intimate images of a real person without their documented consent, and anything illegal where you or your audience live. The content policy has the full list.

Can I use the images commercially?

That depends on each model’s license. The model page lists the terms, so check it before you use an image in paid work.
