Quick answers

Is SpicyLab free to join?

Yes. Creating an account is free, and you can claim a daily coin bonus. You only pay if you choose a plan or a coin pack.

What if I sign up after 24 hours?

You can still join, but the invite won't be linked to your account. Opening your friend's link again starts a fresh 24 hours.

I already have an account. Can I use this invite?

No. Invites only apply to brand-new accounts, so just log in as usual. You can share your own invite link from your account menu.

Does joining through an invite change my price?

No. You pay exactly the same price. Your friend's commission comes from SpicyLab, not from you.

What can my friend see about me?

A masked version of your email, like jo***@gmail.com, and the amount of each purchase that earned them a commission. They never see your creations, your payment details, or your full email.

I opened links from more than one friend. Who gets credit?

The last invite link you opened before creating your account. After you sign up, it can't change.
