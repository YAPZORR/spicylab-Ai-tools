Payment page redesign

Each screen now leads with the plans and the payment button. Plans are bigger, each one has its own color, billing period and savings, and the selected plan is outlined in its color. Benefits moved into a quieter checklist that supports the choice instead of competing with it.

Client feedback“The plan list items are too small to tell them apart. The plan list and payment button are the main elements, while membership benefits are secondary.”
