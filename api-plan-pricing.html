Opens from “How tiers work” on the billing page’s tier chart. Same account: V1, $10 topped up.

How tiers work

Your tier follows the total you’ve topped up, not your remaining balance. $10 now and $20 later puts you at V2.

Cumulative top-ups Tier Reward
Below $10 V0 None
$10 to under $30You’re here V1 None
$30 and above V2 $1 API balance, once
  • The $1 bonus is added once, the first time you reach V2. Later top-ups don’t repeat it, even if a refund takes you below V2 and you reach it again.
  • Bonus balance pays for API tasks only. It can’t be withdrawn and doesn’t count toward your tier.
New account tops up $30$30 + $1 bonus = $31
Account at $10 tops up $20$20 + $1 bonus = $21

Task charges

The second tab of the history section on the billing page. Every task holds its fee first, then charges or releases it.

How charges work

1

Reserve

When a task starts, its fee is held from your available balance.

2

Charge

If the task succeeds, the held amount is charged.

3

Release

If it fails or is rejected, the held amount goes back to your balance.

4

Check

If a task times out and its result is unknown, we confirm what happened before charging or releasing.
